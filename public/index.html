Battery Sizer SA

Upload a NEM12 file from your meter. The page finds your peaks and heaviest days, sizes a battery, inverter and solar array, then shortlists three manufacturers from the dataset.

Your electricity use

Best: a NEM12 CSV from SA Power Networks or your retailer. It is read in this browser and is not uploaded anywhere. No file? Type in the figures from one of your bills instead.

Use the energy bought from the grid, not what the house used from solar. A bill only gives averages, so the busiest day and the highest power are estimated from a typical household pattern with a margin added. Treat the result as a rough guide. Period times follow the tariff section (or the standard times: AM peak 06:00 to 10:00, PM peak 16:00 to midnight, shoulder 10:00 to 16:00, off peak midnight to 06:00).

About the house

Network rules in use are for SA Power Networks: single phase inverters up to 10 kW and 5 kW export, three phase up to 30 kW and 5 kW export per phase.

Your circuits: cooking, air conditioning, hot water (optional, check your switchboard)

Open your switchboard and look for dedicated breakers for each of these. The breaker size (amps) tells us how much power each could draw, which the meter data can hide. Choose whether each should keep running in a blackout. Leave everything on Skip to ignore this section.

Your electricity tariff (optional, from your bill)

Enter the rates from your latest bill, in cents per kWh including GST. The sizing above does not use them yet. They are recorded here for the cost comparison to come. Your entries are remembered in this browser only.

Usage prices and times

Second rows copy the price of the first row until you type a different price into them. Times are 24 hour and can be changed to match your bill. Where a window runs to midnight, enter 00:00 as the end.

Manufacturer ranking and exclusions

Rank 5 is preferred. Excluded manufacturers never appear in the shortlist, whatever their fit. Changes save in this browser. Use Copy settings to keep a version or move it to another browser.

How far to trust this. It is a sizing guide, not a design or a quote. Battery power, solar input and backup limits come from manufacturer datasheets where marked, and are estimated where marked. An accredited installer must confirm the inverter and battery pairing, the backup circuits and network approval.

Solar capacity uses typical Adelaide-area monthly yields, not measured output at the property. Dataset built .
